About GPTPayer Traffic Checker

A free, one-page tool to size up any website.

Type a domain and see its popularity rank, an estimate of how many visits it gets, how old it is, who registered it, its DNS records and whether its server answers. Everything is on one page, with no sign-up.

Where the data comes from

  • Popularity rank: Tranco, a research-based ranking of the top 1 million domains that combines several public lists.
  • WHOIS details: RDAP, the standard registration lookup service.
  • DNS records: Google Public DNS.
  • First seen on the web: the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine.
  • Server status: a request your own browser makes to the site.

How accurate are the traffic numbers?

Visits and ad revenue shown here are rough estimates. We turn a site's rank into a visit count with a simple formula, and revenue assumes $2 to $10 per 1,000 visits. These are not measured figures. Only the site owner, through tools like Google Analytics, can see real visitor numbers.

Rank history covers only the recent weeks that Tranco provides, so the chart range is limited to the data that actually exists. Sites outside the top 1 million are not ranked, so they have no estimate.

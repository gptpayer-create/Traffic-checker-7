Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use gptpayer.online ("the site"). The site is a free tool that shows public information about a website domain you type in.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details to use the checker. The domain you type is processed in your browser and is not stored by us.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages are used. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect information such as your approximate location, device type, browser and the pages you view. This data is used in aggregate. You can learn more at Google's page on how it uses data, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Third-party services used for a check

When you check a domain, your browser contacts these services directly, and they may see your IP address and the domain you typed under their own privacy policies:

  • Tranco (tranco-list.eu) for popularity rank
  • RDAP (rdap.org) for WHOIS registration details
  • Google Public DNS (dns.google) for DNS records
  • Internet Archive (archive.org) for the first saved copy of the site
  • The domain you entered, which your browser contacts to see whether it responds

Advertising

The site may show ads in the future. If it does, ad partners such as Google may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. This page will be updated if ads are added.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We only use cookies set by analytics (and by advertising, if it is added later). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the checker works without them.

Accuracy of data

Traffic figures shown on the site are estimates calculated from public ranking data. They are not measured visits and should not be treated as exact.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to gptpayer@gmail.com.

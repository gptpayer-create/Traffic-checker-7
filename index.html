Questions people ask

How does the traffic checker estimate visits?

It takes the domain's Tranco popularity rank and converts it into a rough daily visit count with a simple formula. The numbers are estimates, not measured traffic.

Why is my website not ranked?

Tranco only ranks the top 1 million domains. New or small sites usually have too little traffic to appear, so no estimate can be shown.

Can I see exact visitors for any site?

No. Only the owner of a site can see exact visitors, using a tool like Google Analytics or Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Where does the data come from?

Rank data comes from Tranco, WHOIS from RDAP, DNS from Google Public DNS and first-seen dates from the Internet Archive. Read more.
